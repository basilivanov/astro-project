## Slice Rules

- Pass means the listed check is green and its API/UI shape matches the documented contract without hidden markdown-only or fallback-only regressions.
- Fallback is acceptable only where the slice explicitly allows degraded success: report section fallback cards and daily feed fallback payloads.
- `VM-NATAL-SUMMARY-REPAIR` is intentionally narrower than live benchmark docs: it tracks deterministic acceptance/fallback behavior, not full OpenRouter editorial stability.
- `VM-BILLING-ACCESS` is rollout-slice proof, not full rollout proof: its pass signal confirms the flag-gated payment/access primitives plus the narrow `natal_master` / `month_forecast` / `year_forecast` / `solar_return` bridge, while catalog-default pricing and the broader product cutover remain intentionally incomplete.
- Untouched areas such as billing, referral, admin, bot delivery, and horary deep-domain flows keep their existing project baseline and were intentionally not re-audited in this pass.
